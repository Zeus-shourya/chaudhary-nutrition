How it all began

The story of Chaudhary Nutrition began with a deeply personal journey. In 2017, Annu Chaudhary's father suffered a severe paralysis attack and was admitted to PGI Rohtak in critical condition. Along with paralysis, he was diagnosed with multiple life-threatening conditions — a damaged pancreas, extremely high blood sugar and blood pressure. Doctors gave him just ten days to live.

But Annu refused to accept defeat. With sheer determination and the power of Ayurvedic remedies, she nursed her father back to a much better state of health, extending his life by eight more years. His recovery inspired many — and became the seed of a greater purpose.

After her father's passing in February 2025, Annu devoted herself entirely to the study of Ayurveda and natural wellness: researching powerful herbs, ancient healing methods and nutrition science to create safe, effective solutions for modern health problems. That passion gave birth to Chaudhary Nutrition — a brand built on love, science and the power of nature.
